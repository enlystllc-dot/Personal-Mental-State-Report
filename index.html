    Transparent by design

    What the numbers mean

    The report leads with the recommended action and the reason it won. Scores describe your answers; mechanism explanations are pattern-level hypotheses. The thresholds remain heuristic, not clinically validated cutoffs.

    ◷

    Sleep pressure

    Sleep duration, recent sleep and current sleepiness are combined as context. Sleep pressure and circadian timing interact; an app cannot measure adenosine.

    ▤

    Cognitive fatigue

    Based on your reported mental effort, clarity and recent task duration. It is a subjective symptom score, not a direct reading of brain performance.

    ⌁

    Reward-seeking / scrolling urge

    The report measures your stated craving and task readiness. Dopamine participates in reward learning and motivation, but no dopamine level is inferred.

    ≈

    Stress / arousal

    Based on reported mental noise and activation. Slow, comfortable breathing may help some people regulate stress, but results vary.

    ↗

    Activity and breaks

    Short breaks can modestly improve subjective vigor and fatigue; overall performance effects are less consistent. Brief movement may help, but it cannot repay sleep debt.

    ⚑

    Recovery debt

    Repeated short sleep or persistent fatigue is treated as a reason to reduce demands and investigate the cause, not as a deficit that meditation alone can fix.

    Decision rules in brief

    1. Safety override: if sleepiness is severe, don't drive or do safety-critical work. A short breathing practice is not a substitute for sleep.
    2. High sleepiness + short/recently restricted sleep: prioritize sleep/rest; consider a brief nap if practical, with time to clear grogginess.
    3. Clear physical need: handle thirst, hunger, bathroom or discomfort first. Illness symptoms change the recommendation toward rest and appropriate medical advice.
    4. High stress / arousal: try gentle paced breathing for 5 minutes. Stop if dizzy or uncomfortable.
    5. High craving + adequate alertness and manageable fatigue: add friction to phone access and start a tiny defined work action.
    6. Moderate fatigue or long work stretch: take a short screen-free break, possibly with a walk.
    7. Nothing clearly dominant: begin a short, low-friction work sprint and reassess rather than endlessly optimizing.
    Safety: the app is not emergency triage. New, severe or persistent fatigue, unexplained daytime sleepiness, loud snoring/gasping, fainting, chest pain, severe shortness of breath, confusion or other concerning symptoms warrant appropriate medical assessment. For an emergency, contact local emergency services.

    Privacy and technical details

    This is a static website. It has no analytics, remote database, login or AI API. Check-ins are saved using localStorage in the browser that you use to access the app. Anyone who can use that browser profile may be able to view the saved data, and exporting it creates a file outside the app. GitHub Pages may log visitor information at the hosting layer; local storage does not mean the entire website has no platform-level logs.

    Scores are not validated clinical scales. “Confidence” describes how directly the user's answers support a behavioral interpretation, not a probability that a neurochemical explanation is correct.
